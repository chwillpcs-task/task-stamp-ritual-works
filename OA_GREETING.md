# LINE OA Greeting — TASK Community (@664ickdm)

Carl's principle (2026-09-23): no instruction card. How stamping works is
discovered through exploring — the counter cards at shops, trying things —
not explained up front. The greeting stays pure delight.

In LINE OA Manager → Greeting message, add three bubbles in this order:
1. Text: `Hello, {name}! 🍦`
2. Photo: `soft-cream-greeting.webp` (tall vanilla swirl cone)
3. Photo: `card-2-more-ice-cream.webp` (vanilla-strawberry swirl cup)
4. Photo: `card-3-ice-cream-menu.webp` (cone on a cafe counter by the menu board)

(Card 1 is the hello + hero shot; card 2 is more ice cream;
card 3 lands on the menu — the quiet invitation to visit.)

Direction agreed 2026-09-23: welcome by name, lead with soft cream and
belonging (not the stamp mechanic). Japan-facing copy leads with
"soft cream"; "soft serve" glossed for Americans only where needed.
